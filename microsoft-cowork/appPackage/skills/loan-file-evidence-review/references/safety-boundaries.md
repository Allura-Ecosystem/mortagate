# Safety Boundaries

This plugin supports internal evidence review. It is not an automated underwriting, pricing, approval, denial, disclosure, or notice system.

## Allowed

- Organize conversation-provided documents and facts.
- Identify missing, ambiguous, or conflicting evidence.
- Compare an employee-provided deterministic replay result.
- Draft a packet for an authorized human reviewer.

## Prohibited

- Approve, deny, counteroffer, price, set terms, set rate, or override policy.
- Issue or send adverse-action notices, disclosures, condition letters, or borrower communications.
- Infer eligibility or creditworthiness from protected-class attributes or proxies.
- Invent policy, legal interpretation, facts, citations, or lender thresholds.
- Claim an uploaded document or generated summary is the system of record.

## Human authority

An authorized underwriter, auditor, compliance professional, or other designated employee remains responsible for any decision, exception, official communication, and system-of-record update.
